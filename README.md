Claim Vault

A user-side on-chain entitlement center for discovering, verifying, and claiming supported rewards on Robinhood Chain.

What is Claim Vault?

Claim Vault helps users find supported on-chain rewards and entitlements associated with their wallet, verify claim details, and safely initiate eligible claims.

Core Features

- Discover supported entitlements
- Verify claim eligibility and details
- Claim supported rewards
- Track verified claim history
- Claim Bundle for compatible claim contracts
- Claim Passport NFT for verified activity

Project Status

Claim Vault is currently in development.

The initial MVP will focus on a small number of verified claim integrations before expanding to additional supported protocols.

Important

Claim Vault is an independent community-built project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Robinhood.
